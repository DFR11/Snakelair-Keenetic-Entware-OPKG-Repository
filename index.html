📷 Smart-Photo

v1.0.39
Порт: :8089 (http://192.168.1.1:8089)

Personal home photo server in Google Photos style right on the router. Reads photos directly from USB drives and generates thumbnails on the fly.

  • Infinite Scroll
  • Interactive scroller of years and dates on the right
  • Full screen viewer, zoom, slideshow, EXIF ​​& GPS
  • Multiple folder selection and Live synchronization
  • 0% CPU load in idle mode (RAM Logging)
